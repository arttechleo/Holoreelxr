## ✨ **Our Solution**

### HoloreelXR: The TikTok of Spatial Computing

**Core Innovation**: Natural hand gestures replace controllers. Scroll, like, heart, and share 3D content just like you do on mobile - but in immersive 3D.

### Key Features

#### 1. **Gesture-First Interface** 🖐️
- **Pinch & Scroll**: Natural vertical feed navigation
- **Heart Gesture**: Save content you love
- **Thumbs Up**: Quick likes
- **Two-Hand Transform**: Rotate and scale 3D objects naturally
- **Grab & Place**: Reposition content in your space

#### 2. **Smart 3D Feed** 📱➡️🥽
- Auto-scaling: Every model fits perfectly
- Spatial persistence: Models stay where you place them
- Smooth transitions: No jarring jumps
- Memory-efficient: Proper cleanup prevents device lag

#### 3. **Content Flexibility** 🎨
- GLTF/GLB models (industry standard)
- Gaussian splats (photorealistic captures)
- Basic shapes (quick prototyping)
- Animations (bring content to life)

#### 4. **WebXR Architecture** 🌐
- **Zero Install**: Works instantly in browser
- **Cross-Platform**: Quest, Vision Pro, Pico, future devices
- **Fast Updates**: Push changes without app review
- **Lower CAC**: No app store friction = lower customer acquisition
